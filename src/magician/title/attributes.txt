;   The title screen's 64 attribute bytes -- 4 rows of 16, covering the
;   nametable two rows at a time in the usual NES order. The file starts at
;   $23C0, which is why the scene descriptor is 960 + 64 bytes rather than 960.
;
;   A byte is %00PPPPPP with the top two bits selecting one of four 4-colour
;   sub-palettes and the low six the palette number within TIT.PAL. The upper
;   halves of rows 0-7 are not used by this screen: the top four rows are solid
;   brick and the row below is the top of the logo, which is drawn in one palette.

EA 75 D5 75 5A A2 AA AA 7D DF FD FF 37 0A 0A 6A ; attr 2
